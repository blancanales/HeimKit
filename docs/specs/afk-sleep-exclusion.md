# AfkSleepExclusion specification

AfkSleepExclusion independently removes inferred-idle players from the native sleep requirement. Its Enabled default is true. It uses the shared activity service and SleepVoteCoordinator described in [architecture](../architecture.md#shared-player-activity) and [MajoritySleep](majority-sleep.md). Without MajoritySleep, every non-idle participant must be in bed. With MajoritySleep, its configured quorum applies after idle exclusion. At least one actual living bed occupant is always required; bed occupants are never excluded, and zero participating players never authorize a time skip.

The module reads server-held character and activity state and changes only the native sleep vote, retaining native afternoon/night, cooldown, bed and sleep broadcast behavior. Unknown/loading actors defer to the native vote only when a living bed occupant is known; otherwise the vote remains false. Death pauses idle inference and does not create a sleeper. Disconnects remove that session; new characters receive fresh grace and activity state. Station/container use holds the player active where observable. Personal-inventory and menu-only activity has blind spots, so idle status is explicitly inferred rather than a claim of human absence.

Settings, lifecycle, Controller processing and Diagnostics formatting remain separate. `[AfkSleepExclusion] Enabled = true` and the [shared activity settings](../server-setup.md#player-activity-and-sleep) require restart after edits. The [hidden debug suppression policy](../server-setup.md#hidden-per-feature-debug-suppression) applies independently. Standard lifecycle/warnings/errors remain visible; optional summaries are limited to once per minute, with separate six-record actor and native-result streams defined below. No standalone AFK toggle or world data is persisted on a player, and no client mod is required. Supported environment is linked from [server setup](../server-setup.md#supported-environment).

## Diagnostic evidence

Effective DebugMode enables bounded optional transition tracking. Actor samples include numeric peer/session identity, state revision, inferred idle, known/alive/bed state, idle duration, last observed signal, recovery reason, grace remaining at capture and sample age. Movement, body rotation, health decrease, equipment change, emote change, leaving bed and authenticated routed method names are observation signals. Concurrent signals use a stable priority for the displayed last signal; the field is not a complete input history. Chat text, packet bodies, equipment identities and inventory contents are never logged.

A new session, unknown-data recovery, character change, observation gap or clock rollback identifies why inference reset. An idle-to-active transition records the observed wake signal. Changes are captured before output throttling and take priority over rotating unchanged snapshots. Up to 4,096 current actor snapshots and 4,096 pending latest states are retained only while effective diagnostics is enabled. Multiple pending states for one peer are replaced by the latest; transition and replacement counts make that loss of intermediate detail explicit. Disconnect/out-of-scope samples have a specific reason. Sample age prevents queued states and captured grace from being mistaken for current state.

At most six actor samples per minute accompany the one-per-minute summary; transition counts, pending changes and overwritten pending states appear in bounded output. Routed observer counters distinguish hook calls, not-ready returns and authenticated activity signals. The native sleep evidence and its independent six-result allowance follow [MajoritySleep](majority-sleep.md#diagnostic-evidence). Missing/false hidden suppression inherits global DebugMode, true suppresses this module's optional history and output. Required activity inference continues for gameplay.
